I am Alexandre Abela, a PhD student in _Graph Machine Learning_ at [École Polytechnique](https://www.polytechnique.edu) in France, under the supervision of [Prof. Johannes Lutzeyer](https://johanneslutzeyer.com). My PhD is carried out under a CIFRE contract, a French scheme that funds doctoral research conducted jointly with a company, and I am hosted as a Research Scientist at [CMA-CGM](https://www.cma-cgm.com/), the 3rd biggest shipping company worldwide. Prior to that, I finished a double master's program with an engineering degree from Ecole Centrale Méditerranée and an artificial intelligence degree from Aix-Marseille Université.

In parallel with my PhD, I am also pursuing a Master's degree in Theoretical Physics at Aix-Marseille Université. Beyond a genuine love for physics, this is motivated by two aspects that feed directly into my research: building a deeper understanding of quantum physics to support my work on quantum machine learning, and exploring quantum field theory, where symmetries and group theory play a central role, concepts that carry over naturally to geometric deep learning. More broadly, I am very interested to the growing interplay between physics and machine learning.

Research interests:

- **Graph Machine Learning** : Fundamental work on _Graph Neural Networks_ to relax the permutation equivariance assumption of these architectures.
- **Quantum Machine Learning** : Leveraging QML for graph machine learning to go beyond the deterministic limit of the Weisfeiler-Lehman test for GNNs.
