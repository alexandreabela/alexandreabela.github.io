Projects

  • Theoretical Analysis of Quantum Graph Neural Networks [team project]
    • Theoretical analysis of the expressive power of quantum graph neural networks (QGNNs) and comparison with classical GNNs.
    • Proof that QGNNs can be more powerful than classical GNNs in distinguishing non-isomorphic graphs, and identification of specific classes of graphs where this advantage is most pronounced.
    • Analysis of the computational complexity of QGNNs and identification of potential bottlenecks in their implementation.
  • DQN Exploration Improvement using GANs
    • Working on the Pokémon Red game environment using Python to improve the exploration in Deep-Q networks using the discriminator of a trained GAN.
    • Implementation of a Deep-Q network agent using rllib and customization of the training phase program to add the discriminator of the GAN in the determination of whether the new state has already been explored before or not.
    • Training a GAN from scratch using images of the already visited states by the agent.
  • Multimodal Learning for turn-taking prediction in dialogs [team project]
    • Preprocessing and training of models on unimodal data for turn-taking prediction using LSTM models for audio, CamemBERT for text, and ResNet for videos.
    • Comparison between early fusion architecture with dense neural networks and late fusion architecture with the trained unimodal models.
  • Airbus Hackathon Text Summarization (3rd place out of 25 teams)
    • Use of classical transformers trained on text summarization tasks like T5 and BART to produce summaries using bootstrapping strategies to choose the summary from the model that has the highest similarity with the original text (similarity calculated with SentenceBERT).
    • Then use of an ensemble strategy to choose the summary with again the highest similarity between the outputs of T5 and BART.
